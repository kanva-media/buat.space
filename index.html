FAQ

Before we start.

Do I need to write all the website content?

No. We ask structured questions about your business, customers and services. We use your answers to organise and refine the website content.

Can I give you a website I like?

Yes. Share 2–3 references and tell us what you like about each one. We use them to understand your preferred direction rather than copy them.

Do you use AI to build the website?

Yes. AI is part of our production workflow. It helps us move faster, but every website is reviewed, tested and refined before delivery.

How long does the project take?

The first draft is targeted within 2 working days after all required materials are received. Final delivery is targeted within 5 working days after your consolidated feedback.

What if I need more than the standard package?

Additional pages, advanced functionality, e-commerce, booking systems and other custom requirements can be quoted separately.

Can you maintain the website after launch?

Yes. A separate monthly website care plan can cover hosting management, monitoring and a defined amount of minor updates.
